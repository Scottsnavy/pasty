Chapter One

The Foundation

The light in the garden was doing exactly what we hoped it would. Everything felt still, yet moving all at once.

It began not with a grand gesture, but with the quiet realization that certain moments are built to endure. We looked at the horizon—a soft blend of moss and oak—and understood that our story was being etched into the very landscape of New England.

The air carried the scent of heritage. There is a specific kind of weight to history when you are standing in it; a gravity that pulls at the present and anchors it to the past.
